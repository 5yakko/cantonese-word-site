Common questions

How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Cantonese Word. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then the widget area under the clock.

When does the word change?
At midnight, your local time. Day one is the day you first opened the app.

The pronunciation button doesn't appear.
It uses your iPhone's Cantonese (Hong Kong) voice. Add it in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Chinese (Hong Kong).

Can I see words I missed?
Swipe to Past Words. Every earlier day is there, and you can search by character or meaning.

Can I see the illustrations in colour?
Yes: Settings → Colour illustrations.
