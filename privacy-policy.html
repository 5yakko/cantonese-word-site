Privacy Policy

Cantonese Word · Last updated 21 October 2026

Cantonese Word does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What the app keeps, and where

The app remembers a few things so it works the way you set it up: the date you first opened it (so it knows which word is today's), the words you've marked as favourites, and your settings. All of this stays on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking
  • No internet connection: the app makes no network requests

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the reminder is scheduled on your iPhone by iOS itself. No notification data leaves your device.

Feedback form

The "Report a mistake" link opens a Google Form in your browser, outside the app. Only what you choose to type there is sent: the word, your note, and your email address if you want a reply. It is used only to fix mistakes and answer you, and is never shared or sold. Google's own privacy policy covers Google Forms.

Children

Because the app collects no information at all, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app is.

Contact

Questions: use the feedback form.
